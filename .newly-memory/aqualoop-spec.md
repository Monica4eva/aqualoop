AquaLoop ("Order. Deliver. Return. Repeat.") is a demo app for a small drinks-delivery business, built from the user's long spec, with Customer, Owner and Driver roles and no passwords.
**Why:** the user asked for the design pictures in design/ (home, catalogue, deliveries, containers) to be matched exactly; when a picture and the guidance disagree, the picture wins.
**How to apply:** the app is always light, text is smaller than the starter's, and everything the pictures show must fit above the tab bar. Messages are read on the phone by the "Demo parser" (the NVIDIA second check is a placeholder). All data is fictional. Out of scope: payments, real WhatsApp, maps, other languages.
