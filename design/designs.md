## Set 1 · 2026-11-06 · picked Clean Modern, built
Brief: AquaLoop runs the daily operations of a small drinks delivery business, connecting customers, owners, and drivers. The home screen for the customer role features a chat interface for ordering water and milk, a repeat order manager, and an empty container tracker. It is designed to make ordering and inventory management seamless.

3. Playful Craft: A warm illustrated look with friendly rounded shapes, pastel cream backgrounds, and expressive custom icons for every drink type.. design/sets/2026-11-06-1/3-playful-craft.jpg
